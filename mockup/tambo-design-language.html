tambo

The waystation for your models
design language — rebrand of llama-desk v1 · 2026-11

A sturdier shelter for llama.cpp.

Tambo is the rebrand of llama-desk: the same single Go binary that queues GGUFs, supervises llama-server processes, and routes OpenAI-compatible traffic — now with a visual identity that says what it is. An inn on the road between your models and their callers.

The language is deliberately ordinary where ordinary wins — tables, forms, logs — and committed where it counts: one madder-orange accent, a warm brown-black dye ground, corners that stay hard, and status you can read at a glance from across the room.

01 Color

Five dye lots and a warm gray. Dark is primary — the panel lives next to a terminal, not in a sunlit office. Click any chip to copy its hex.

02 Type

Inter for everything you read and press; JetBrains Mono for everything a machine emitted — filenames, ports, argument vectors, byte counts. Mono is a data signal, never a style.

Display / 40 / 800 / -0.03em Your models. Your server.
H2 / 24 / 700 / -0.02em Servers on this host
H3 / 16 / 600 / -0.01em Launch arguments
Body / 15 / 400 / 1.6 Transfers resume where they stopped: a cancelled download keeps its .part file and revalidates the ETag on the next attempt.
Caption / 12 / 400 mono GET /v1/chat/completions → model "qwen-main" → 127.0.0.1:8081

Numerals

All byte counts, ports, PIDs, and percentages are set in mono with tabular-nums, so columns of numbers rule up. Example: 4.2 GB · 22 MB/s · pid 31415 · :8081

03 Shape & depth

No soft pillows. The corner ceiling is 4 px on controls and 6 px on surfaces; status squares keep a hard edge, echoing the doorway of the mark. Depth comes from real shadows — offset and blurred — never from glows around idle things.

Gateway

OpenAI-compatible traffic, routed by the request's model field.

GET /v1/models → live inventory 2 served
POST /v1/chat/completions → "qwen-main" → 127.0.0.1:8081
POST /v1/completions → "qwen-main" → 127.0.0.1:8081
POST /v1/embeddings → "mistral-small" not running

SSE streams and error statuses pass through untouched. A caller disconnecting cancels the upstream request. Auth headers are stripped before forwarding.

06 Motion

One authored moment: the doorway in the mark lights when the panel opens — a tambo with the lamp on. Everything else moves once, quickly, and stops. Hover the tracks.

--t-t-fast · 120ms — hover, press, color
--t-t-med · 200ms — theme, toggle, toast
--t-t-slow · 320ms — progress, pane crossfade

All easing is cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1): fast out, settle soft. Nothing bounces, nothing parallaxes, and prefers-reduced-motion stills every loop.
